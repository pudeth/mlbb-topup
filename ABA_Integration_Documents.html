1. Processing Flow of the Website

Below is the step-by-step technical and user journey for processing transactions via the ABA PayWay integration on our platform.

Step 1: Product Selection & User Input
The customer visits our website, selects the desired digital product (e.g., Mobile Legends Diamonds), and enters their unique in-game Player ID and Zone ID. The system validates the Player ID format.
Step 2: Checkout Initiation
The customer selects "ABA PayWay" as their preferred payment method. Our backend server securely generates an Order ID and requests a payment session from the ABA PayWay API.
Step 3: Payment Authorization (ABA PayWay)
The customer is presented with the ABA PayWay checkout interface (either via deep-link to the ABA Mobile app or a dynamic QR code on desktop). The customer authorizes the exact USD amount.
Step 4: Payment Confirmation (Webhook)
Upon successful payment, the ABA PayWay system sends a secure Server-to-Server callback (Webhook) to our backend API confirming the transaction status as "PAID".
Step 5: Automated Digital Fulfillment
Once our backend receives the official confirmation from ABA, the system instantly triggers an API request to our digital goods supplier to dispatch the diamonds directly to the customer's game account.
Step 6: Receipt & Order Completion
The customer's screen automatically transitions to a "Payment Successful" receipt. If the digital supplier is temporarily out of balance, the order is safely held in an "Awaiting Balance" state, and an administrator is alerted to manually approve the fulfillment once funds are replenished.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Digital Goods Delivery

Our platform provides direct top-up services for digital game credits (Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Diamonds). Due to the immediate and automated nature of digital goods delivery, all sales are final once the transaction is completed and the digital goods have been credited to the customer's game account.

2. Cancellations

Customers may cancel their order at any point before completing the payment via the ABA PayWay gateway. Once the payment is authorized and processed, the order cannot be canceled as the system automatically dispatches the digital goods to the provided Player ID.

3. Refund Conditions

We will only issue a full refund under the following circumstances:

  • The payment was successfully deducted from the customer's bank account, but the digital goods (diamonds) were not delivered due to a system error or supplier outage.
  • The customer provided an invalid Player ID, causing the delivery to fail permanently, and the funds are held in our system.

4. Refund Processing

If a refund is eligible, the customer must contact our support team within 48 hours of the transaction, providing the Order ID and ABA transaction receipt. Eligible refunds will be credited back to the original ABA account used for the purchase within 3 to 5 business days.
